# apps/api

Fastify backend: REST under `/v1`, the only writer to the database, plus pg-boss background jobs.

- `pnpm dev:api` (root) runs it locally with `.env`; Node runs the TypeScript directly.
- Every route needs a Supabase login unless it sets `config: { public: true }`, and every route must declare a zod `response` schema (startup fails otherwise).
- Errors always look like `{ "error": { "code", "message", "requestId", "details?" } }` (`apiErrorSchema` in `@outfitly/shared`).
- Logged-in writes (POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE) need an `Idempotency-Key` header; repeats replay the first response.
- Jobs: `src/jobs.ts` (`system-ping` test job, daily `idempotency-cleanup`, `media-process`, `event-partitions` at startup and daily, `search-terms` at startup and every 15 minutes, `photo-spot` and `photo-fingerprint` for look photos).
- Photos (`src/media`, `src/routes/media.ts`): `POST /v1/media/uploads` → PUT the original to the signed R2 link → `POST /v1/media/:id/complete` → job makes 1080/600/300 WebP (EXIF/GPS stripped, HEIC via libheif WASM) plus thumbhash and dominant colour → `GET /v1/media/:id` shows the links. `pnpm media:try photo.heic` runs a real photo through R2 and prints the 3 links.
- Photo AI (`src/ai`, Step 3.3): when one of a look's photos is ready, `photo-spot` sends our 1080 copy to Gemini (`GEMINI_MODEL`, plain `fetch`, JSON schema in `src/ai/spot.ts`, prompt version `SPOT_PROMPT_VERSION`) once: garments with boxes (fractions of the photo), a sentence and search words for the look, safety flags. A flagged photo's looks are hidden and it gets a `moderation_queue` item. Then `photo-fingerprint` asks services/embedder for the whole photo's and each garment's fingerprint (20 boxes per call). Every Gemini call is logged in `ai_calls` with tokens and cost; at `AI_DAILY_CAP_USD` (UTC day) jobs pause until midnight. Without `GEMINI_API_KEY` only fingerprints are made; without `EMBEDDER_URL`/`EMBEDDER_SECRET` none. A look created from photos that are already ready must queue them itself (`queuePhotoAi`).
- Shop links (`src/links`, `src/routes/links.ts`): `POST /v1/links/resolve` `{ url }` (login, idempotency key, 20 per minute per login) reads a pasted shop link: follows its redirects (up to 5, each checked), reads the product from the page's schema.org JSON-LD, then Open Graph / product meta tags (name, brand, barcode, photo, price, currency, stock), and creates or updates the product and offer. The posted link is kept exactly (affiliate tags and all) as `offers.url`; the shop's own page, tracking removed, is `offers.page_url`. A link read in the last hour is answered from the database. Jobs `product-photo` (copies the shop photo into R2 as an ownerless photo, same steps as an upload) and `product-fingerprint` follow. Every fetch: public addresses only (checked on the connection's own name lookup), http(s) on ports 80/443, 3 s to connect, 10 s in total, pages read up to 3 MB, photos up to 25 MB. Problems come back as 422 `link_unreadable` with `details.problem` (`LINK_PROBLEMS` in packages/shared). Microlink is a fallback for pages without product data only, behind the `link_reader_microlink` flag (off). `pnpm links:try <links…> | --file links.txt [--save] [--out report.md]` prints what each link gives and the success rate (without `--save` nothing is written).
- Link check (`src/links/check.ts`, Step 3.5): job `link-check` decides each pending look item once. Sold out → rejected (`sold_out`); the garment (the poster's pick, else the smallest piece under their dot, else the look's piece most like the product, preferring the product's category and pieces no other item shows) is compared with the product photo by fingerprint (same model only): from `verifyAt` → verified, below `rejectBelow` → rejected (`not_similar`), in between Gemini sees the cut-out piece and the product photo (`link-check-v1`, logged in `ai_calls`, under the daily cap): yes → verified, no → rejected (`ai_different`), unsure or refused → review (`moderation_queue` kind `link_check`, look item status `review`). No usable shop photo or no piece found → review. Missing parts (photo not read, fingerprints, no Gemini key) leave it pending: the photo and product jobs queue waiting checks when they finish, and `link-check-sweep` (hourly) asks again about up to 500. Thresholds live in the `settings` row `link_check_thresholds` (read once a minute). `addLookItem` puts an offer on a look and queues its check (Phase 4's posting uses it); a link whose product changes puts its items back to pending. Every decision is a `link_checks` row; reason codes are `LINK_CHECK_REASONS` (packages/shared), with the poster's messages under `linkCheck.*` in the app's translations.
- `pnpm seed:items <folder>` adds the `links` lists in the seed folder's looks.json to their looks and checks them right away (reads the links, copies and fingerprints product photos, reads unread look photos with Gemini); links taken out of the file come off the look. `pnpm links:calibrate --make-page` writes `~/Downloads/outfitly-link-pairs/index.html` (garment + product pairs to label Same / Different, "Copy CSV"); `--file pairs.csv [--ai]` reports wrong items auto-verified (goal under 5%), good links rejected and the share sent to the AI, with suggested thresholds; `--set <verifyAt> <rejectBelow>` changes them.
- `pnpm ai:spot-check [--count 50] [--author outfitly.picks] [--again]` reads that many looks' cover photos (skipping ones already read), fingerprints them, prints the cost per 1,000 photos and writes `~/Downloads/outfitly-spot-check/index.html`, which draws the boxes and adds up your right / wrong / missed ticks.
- Seed looks (dev only, never production; folder paths are relative to the repo root):
  - `pnpm seed:looks ./photos --init` writes `looks.json` with one look per photo; fill in `caption`, `tags` (any of the occasions/seasons in `@outfitly/shared`), `author` (handle; created as a creator profile if new, optional `authorName`). Put several file names in one `photos` list for a multi-photo look (first = cover).
  - `pnpm seed:looks ./photos` runs every photo through the real pipeline and creates published looks. The file is the source of truth: re-running updates instead of duplicating (a photo is known by its content, a look by its first photo), brings back seed looks that were hidden, and retires (soft-deletes) older looks of these photos that the file no longer has, e.g. after grouping photos or changing a cover. The first look in the file is the newest. Photos over 25 MB are refused before upload.
  - `pnpm seed:fake-looks [--count N]` adds speed-test looks (posters `@loadtest.01`–`20`, gradient placeholders) up to N (default 10,000); `--remove` deletes them all.
  - Both rebuild search's vocabulary when done.
- Search (`src/search.ts`, public): `GET /v1/search/looks?q=` (results, 20 per page), `/v1/search/suggest?q=` (while typing: searches and people), `/v1/search/popular` (tags for the chips). Looks carry a generated `search_vector` (tags + caption weigh A, AI description and AI search words B; `'simple'` config, no stemming). Typos and Swedish tag words are matched against the `search_terms` vocabulary with pg_trgm (job `search-terms`, every 15 minutes and at startup). `pnpm search:speed` times it against staging.
- Profiles (`src/profile-page.ts`, public): `GET /v1/profiles/:id` (counts, following) and `/v1/profiles/:id/looks` (newest first).
- Deploy: `fly deploy . --config apps/api/fly.toml --dockerfile apps/api/Dockerfile` from the repo root. Migrations run as the release command.
