# apps/api

Fastify backend: REST under `/v1`, the only writer to the database, plus pg-boss background jobs.

- `pnpm dev:api` (root) runs it locally with `.env`; Node runs the TypeScript directly.
- Every route needs a Supabase login unless it sets `config: { public: true }`, and every route must declare a zod `response` schema (startup fails otherwise).
- Errors always look like `{ "error": { "code", "message", "requestId", "details?" } }` (`apiErrorSchema` in `@outfitly/shared`).
- Logged-in writes (POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE) need an `Idempotency-Key` header; repeats replay the first response.
- Jobs: `src/jobs.ts` (`system-ping` test job, daily `idempotency-cleanup`, `media-process`, `event-partitions` at startup and daily, `search-terms` at startup and every 15 minutes, `photo-spot` and `photo-fingerprint` for look photos).
- Photos (`src/media`, `src/routes/media.ts`): `POST /v1/media/uploads` → PUT the original to the signed R2 link → `POST /v1/media/:id/complete` → job makes 1080/600/300 WebP (EXIF/GPS stripped, HEIC via libheif WASM) plus thumbhash and dominant colour → `GET /v1/media/:id` shows the links. `pnpm media:try photo.heic` runs a real photo through R2 and prints the 3 links.
- Photo AI (`src/ai`, Step 3.3): when one of a look's photos is ready, `photo-spot` sends our 1080 copy to Gemini (`GEMINI_MODEL`, plain `fetch`, JSON schema in `src/ai/spot.ts`, prompt version `SPOT_PROMPT_VERSION`) once: garments with boxes (fractions of the photo), a sentence and search words for the look, safety flags. A flagged photo's looks are hidden and it gets a `moderation_queue` item. Then `photo-fingerprint` asks services/embedder for the whole photo's and each garment's fingerprint (20 boxes per call). Every Gemini call is logged in `ai_calls` with tokens and cost; at `AI_DAILY_CAP_USD` (UTC day) jobs pause until midnight. Without `GEMINI_API_KEY` only fingerprints are made; without `EMBEDDER_URL`/`EMBEDDER_SECRET` none. A look created from photos that are already ready must queue them itself (`queuePhotoAi`).
- Shop links (`src/links`, `src/routes/links.ts`): `POST /v1/links/resolve` `{ url }` (login, idempotency key, 20 per minute per login) reads a pasted shop link: follows its redirects (up to 5, each checked), reads the product from the page's schema.org JSON-LD, then Open Graph / product meta tags (name, brand, barcode, photo, price, currency, stock), and creates or updates the product and offer. The posted link is kept exactly (affiliate tags and all) as `offers.url`; the shop's own page, tracking removed, is `offers.page_url`. A link read in the last hour is answered from the database. Jobs `product-photo` (copies the shop photo into R2 as an ownerless photo, same steps as an upload) and `product-fingerprint` follow. Every fetch: public addresses only (checked on the connection's own name lookup), http(s) on ports 80/443, 3 s to connect, 10 s in total, pages read up to 3 MB, photos up to 25 MB. Problems come back as 422 `link_unreadable` with `details.problem` (`LINK_PROBLEMS` in packages/shared). Microlink is a fallback for pages without product data only, behind the `link_reader_microlink` flag (off). `pnpm links:try <links…> | --file links.txt [--save] [--out report.md]` prints what each link gives and the success rate (without `--save` nothing is written).
- Link check (`src/links/check.ts`, Step 3.5): job `link-check` decides each pending look item once. Gone → rejected (`link_removed`); sold out → rejected (`sold_out`). The piece: the poster's pick (`garment_id`), else the smallest piece under their dot (none → review `dot_not_on_piece`), else, once every photo in scope is read, the look's piece most like the product (its category first, pieces no other item uses first); the check's piece is stored as `matched_garment_id`. Compared with the product photo by fingerprint (same model only): from `verifyAt` → verified, below `rejectBelow` → rejected (`not_similar`), in between Gemini sees the cut-out piece and the product photo (`link-check-v1`, minimal thinking, 512 output tokens, logged in `ai_calls` with the look item, under the daily cap): yes → verified, no → rejected (`ai_different`), unsure or refused → review (`ai_unsure`, also after two paid answers we couldn't use). No usable shop photo → review (`no_product_photo`); no piece → review (`no_garment`); a fingerprint, same-model fingerprints or an accepted AI request still missing after 3 days → review (`check_failed`). Review = look item status `review` + an open `moderation_queue` item (kind `link_check`), closed when the item is checked again, decided or removed (`removeLookItems`). A decision is only saved if the item's offer, product, product photo and poster's pick didn't change meanwhile. Missing parts leave it pending: the photo and product jobs queue waiting checks when they finish, and `link-check-sweep` (hourly) asks again about up to 500, least recently tried first. Thresholds live in the `settings` row `link_check_thresholds` (read once a minute). `addLookItem` puts an offer on a look and queues its check (Phase 4's posting uses it); a link whose product changes puts its items back to pending (`recheckLookItems`). Every decision is a `link_checks` row; reason codes are `LINK_CHECK_REASONS` (packages/shared, also a database check), with the poster's messages under `linkCheck.*` in the app's translations.
- `pnpm seed:items <folder>` adds the `links` lists in the seed folder's looks.json to their looks and checks them right away (reads the links, copies and fingerprints product photos, reads unread look photos with Gemini); links taken out of the file come off the look. `pnpm links:calibrate --make-page` writes `~/Downloads/outfitly-link-pairs/index.html` (garment + product pairs to label Same / Different, "Copy CSV"); `--file pairs.csv [--ai]` reports wrong items auto-verified (goal under 5%; PASS only with `--ai` and 100 pairs), good links rejected and the share sent to the AI, with suggested thresholds; `--set <verifyAt> <rejectBelow>` changes them (verifyAt 0.5–1, rejectBelow from 0 up to verifyAt). `seed:items` makes at most one request a second per shop, and a link that fails to read keeps its item.
- `pnpm ai:spot-check [--count 50] [--author outfitly.picks] [--again]` reads that many looks' cover photos (skipping ones already read), fingerprints them, prints the cost per 1,000 photos and writes `~/Downloads/outfitly-spot-check/index.html`, which draws the boxes and adds up your right / wrong / missed ticks.
- Seed looks (dev only, never production; folder paths are relative to the repo root):
  - `pnpm seed:looks ./photos --init` writes `looks.json` with one look per photo; fill in `caption`, `tags` (any of the occasions/seasons in `@outfitly/shared`), `author` (handle; created as a creator profile if new, optional `authorName`). Put several file names in one `photos` list for a multi-photo look (first = cover).
  - `pnpm seed:looks ./photos` runs every photo through the real pipeline and creates published looks. The file is the source of truth: re-running updates instead of duplicating (a photo is known by its content, a look by its first photo), brings back seed looks that were hidden, and retires (soft-deletes) older looks of these photos that the file no longer has, e.g. after grouping photos or changing a cover. The first look in the file is the newest. Photos over 25 MB are refused before upload.
  - `pnpm seed:fake-looks [--count N]` adds speed-test looks (posters `@loadtest.01`–`20`, gradient placeholders) up to N (default 10,000); `--remove` deletes them all.
  - Both rebuild search's vocabulary when done.
- Search (`src/search.ts`, public): `GET /v1/search/looks?q=` (results, 20 per page: looks whose words match, merged by reciprocal rank fusion with looks whose cover looks like the words — the words' fingerprint from the fingerprint service, 1.5 s at most, kept in memory for the last 500 searches; without an answer, words alone; up to 400 per search, saved as a `look_lists` list and reused for 15 minutes), `/v1/search/suggest?q=` (while typing: searches and people), `/v1/search/popular` (tags for the chips). Looks carry a generated `search_vector` (tags + caption weigh A, AI description and AI search words B; `'simple'` config, no stemming). Typos and Swedish tag words are matched against the `search_terms` vocabulary with pg_trgm (job `search-terms`, every 15 minutes and at startup). `pnpm search:speed` times it against staging.
- "More like this" and "similar pieces" (Step 3.8, public): `GET /v1/looks/:lookId/similar` (src/similar-looks.ts): the looks whose cover fingerprints are nearest (pgvector HNSW, iterative scan; same model only), never the look itself, the viewer's own, "see less" ones or ones seen in the last day, seen-this-week ones last, For You's diversity rules; looks without a fingerprint get looks sharing their tags. Page 1 saves the list (`look_lists`, up to 400; logged-out lists shared per look for 15 minutes), later pages read on from it. `GET /v1/look-items/:itemId/similar?sort=nearest|cheaper` (src/similar-pieces.ts): products whose shop photos look most like a shown piece (as worn: its matched garment's fingerprint, else the product's), same kind, with our photo and a shop link that is a shown piece of a shown look and not sold out; `cheaper` sorts the nearest 60 by price, the piece's currency first. `pnpm ai:fingerprint` fingerprints look photos that have none (no Gemini needed). `pnpm similar:speed [--direct [--database <url>]]` times both (and search); `pnpm similar:speed-data` builds a local 10,000-look catalogue from the seed fingerprints for it; `pnpm similar:rate` writes `~/Downloads/outfitly-similar-rating/index.html` to judge 20 looks' "More like this" by eye.
- Profiles (`src/profile-page.ts`, public): `GET /v1/profiles/:id` (counts, following) and `/v1/profiles/:id/looks` (newest first).
- Deploy: `fly deploy . --config apps/api/fly.toml --dockerfile apps/api/Dockerfile` from the repo root. Migrations run as the release command.
