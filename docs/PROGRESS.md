- 2026-11-03 — Saving rules (Step 2.4, Pinterest-like):
  - A look on a board is always saved too: adding it to a board saves it, and unsaving takes it off every board.
  - Removing a look from one board keeps it saved. Deleting a board keeps its looks saved.
  - A look can be on several boards. Board names are 1–50 characters (spaces tidied, no line breaks); a profile can have up to 200 boards; duplicate names are allowed (so a guest merge never clashes).
  - Saves come newest first; saving again after unsaving puts the look back on top, and saving an already-saved look changes nothing.
  - Elias's choices: double-tap saves on the look page only (on the grid it would slow every tap that opens a look by about ¼ s); no Share item in the menu until sharing works; `expo-haptics` approved for the vibration.
- 2026-11-03 — Offline saving (Step 2.4): every save, unsave, board change and "see less" goes into a list on the phone (MMKV, up to 500), sent one at a time and in order, each with its own Idempotency-Key reused on every retry. Retries: 2 s, doubling to at most 30 s, plus when the app comes back to the front (no network-status library needed: reconnecting is noticed within ~30 s). Refusals that can't change (e.g. the look was deleted) are dropped; 403/409 are retried up to 10 times. Screens show the result straight away (a waiting action wins over cached data), and Saved shows waiting saves even if it never loaded. Creating, renaming and deleting boards needs a connection (the screens say so).
- 2026-11-03 — The sign-up moment (Step 2.4): a save while logged out makes the guest account (with the CAPTCHA) and opens the sign-up sheet, once per launch. After that, guests see "Sign up to keep your saves" in Saved. A sign-in waits for a guest account still being made, and no guest may start while a sign-in is running; before the guest's content moves to an account, a save already on its way lands first. "See less" alone never makes an account (it waits for a login). Only a brand-new guest carries the logged-out For You over; signing in to an existing account loads that account's own feed.
- 2026-11-03 — "See less like this": the look leaves the cached feeds at once and new For You lists; looks with the same occasion + season tags as the profile's 50 newest "see less" looks go to the back of each pool (AI similarity replaces the tags in Phase 3).
- 2026-11-03 — Signing out with saves that haven't reached the server (offline after a 3 s try) asks first: they would be lost.
- 2026-11-03 — Test login for Maestro: a Supabase user made for tests (`eliascroesus+outfitly-test@gmail.com`, password in `.env` as `TEST_LOGIN_PASSWORD`, never in git), signed in with a developer-only email + password form. Apple needs the developer account and email codes need an email service (BEFORE_LAUNCH), so the automated test can't use those.
- 2026-11-03 — Impressions (Step 2.3): a card counts when at least half of it has been on screen for 1 second without a break, once per card per list (a refresh starts counting again); paused while Home isn't on screen or the app is in the background. Events wait on the phone (up to 500, survive closing the app) and go out in batches: at 20, 10 s after the first, or when the app goes to the background. Logged-out visitors send a random id for the app install (`session_id`); nothing else identifies them.
- 2026-11-03 — `POST /v1/events` works logged out and skips the Idempotency-Key table: each event carries an id made on the phone and is stored with the phone's time, so a resent batch lands on the same (time, id) and is skipped by the database (saves two writes per batch per user). Times more than 7 days old or 10 minutes ahead are dropped; only published looks are stored; 50 events per batch, 20 batches a minute per IP logged out and 60 logged in (the app sends one every 10 s at most). After a failed send the phone waits 30 s, then twice as long after each further failure up to 15 minutes (+ up to 25% at random), sending nothing in between except one try when the app goes to the background (found in review: it used to send on every new impression during an outage).
- 2026-11-03 — Popularity counts **viewers**, not impressions: one per look per person (profile, or app install when logged out) per day. Found in review: reopening the app re-counted the same looks, and the public events endpoint let anyone push a look's score down with repeats. Score = (decayed saves + 1) / (decayed viewer-days + 20). Faking many app installs is still possible within the rate limit (see "Later"). A logged-in profile's impressions also fill `seen` (For You leaves those looks out for 7 days); `seen` rows older than 7 days are deleted daily (`seen-cleanup`, 03:47 UTC).
- 2026-11-03 — Home grid: the next page loads 60% of the way into the newest page; photos for the next two screens are fetched ahead (the 300/600/1080 size that is sharp at the card's real pixel width — 600 on today's phones); cards keep the photo's shape between 0.6 and 1.8 × as tall as wide (beyond that the photo is cropped). Feeds are never refetched behind the user's back (that would mix a new first page with the old list's cursors): a new list only comes from pull to refresh or the cold-start refresh. The phone stores the first 2 pages of each feed; at a cold start they show at once and a fresh list replaces them only if the user hasn't touched the grid yet (then it jumps to the top before the screen is drawn). Page requests TanStack Query no longer needs are cancelled.
- 2026-11-03 — Tapping a card opens a bare look screen (photo + back) so the "opens a look" test works now; the real look page is Step 2.5. The card's save button is visible but does nothing until Step 2.4.

- 2026-11-03 — For You is public (logged-out visitors get the fresh + popular mix); Following needs a login. A guest session only starts at the first save, so most first-time browsing is logged out.
- 2026-11-03 — For You saves its ranked list (`feed_snapshots`, up to 400 looks = 20 pages, deleted after a day); pages read from it by position. Logged-out visitors share one of 8 lists made in the last 15 minutes (found in review: one list per anonymous visit let anyone fill the database, ~6.5 KB a refresh); a profile keeps its 5 newest lists. - 2026-11-03 — Following = newest first, everything from people you follow (Instagram-style): no diversity rules and no "seen" filter there (with one followed person, "never the same author twice" would hide all but one look). Keyset cursor on (published_at, id) with microseconds.
- 2026-11-03 — "Seen" = the card was actually on screen: Step 2.3's impression logging writes `seen`; the feed only reads it. Served-but-never-scrolled looks aren't burned (the catalogue is small at launch).

- 2026-11-03 — Each new For You list reshuffles the popular and taste pools slightly (score × 0.75–1 random), so refreshing doesn't show the identical feed.
- 2026-11-03 — Popularity score: (saves + 1) / (impressions + 20), both decayed with a 7-day half-life over 30 days; written only when the rounded score changes, and without touching `updated_at` (the score is derived; `updated_at` stays "last edited", e.g. for sitemaps later).

## Later (parked ideas, not in current scope)
- Measure scrolling on a real mid-range Android phone with FlashList's `useBenchmark` (JS frame rate, blank area) once there is one.
- Events are credited to the profile active when the batch is sent, not when the card was seen (the queue survives sign-out and, from Step 4.1, profile switching). Store the profile per event and send batches per profile before Step 4.1. The same goes for the saves outbox (Step 2.4): it sends as whoever is active at send time; store the profile per action before profile switching exists (a retry under another profile would be refused with 422 and dropped).
- Idempotency: answers below 500 are stored and replayed for 24 h, including a 403 that might pass later; consider freeing the key for 403/409/429 like 5xx (the outbox drops an action after 10 refusals).
- Guest merge: a save sent as the guest that reaches the database after the merge started could stay on the closed guest profile (the app now waits for its in-flight save first, so the window is tiny). A server-side fix: the merge locks the guest's memberships and save/board writes take a share lock on the acting membership.
- "See less like this" has no undo yet (Pinterest shows one in the message).
- The install id sent with logged-out events is a lasting identifier sent before any consent: review with the legal step (5.5).
- Phones whose clock is more than 10 minutes ahead lose their events (dropped by the server) and the app doesn't notice; report to Sentry when far fewer are accepted than sent.
- A failed pull to refresh is silent (the old list stays); show a short message once there's a toast pattern.
- Cards under the floating iOS tab bar count as visible for impressions (they are partly visible through it).
- The hidden feed (For You / Following switch) uses `display: none`; on the new React Native architecture its cards are rebuilt when shown again. Fine in the simulator check; watch for flashing on a real Android phone.
- Impressions from the public events endpoint could be faked to push a look's popularity down (only the rate limit stops it). When rollups arrive (Step 4.4), count unique viewers per look per day instead of raw impressions.
- Popularity reads impressions straight from `events` (30 days); switch it to the daily rollups (Step 4.4) once events grow large.
- Taste pool: a pick that few looks carry makes it read the whole catalogue (165 ms p95 at 10,000 looks from the Mac, grows with the catalogue). Once style tags exist, add a GIN index on the tags (or cap the scan) before the catalogue gets large.
- Popularity job at scale: it reads all impression events of 30 days every 15 minutes (no index on `events(name, created_at)`/`look_id`) and every score change rewrites `looks_feed_popular_idx` (index bloat); move to rollups and watch bloat as traffic grows.
- Logged-out visitors have no `seen`, so their refresh only differs by the light reshuffle; consider a device-local "seen" list sent with page 1 if this feels stale.
- Fake looks share 30 pictures, so they're fine for API timing (2.2) but not a fair "no blank cards while fast scrolling" test (2.3): use the real looks or a much bigger pool there.
